"Ingrid Solberg-Mbeki has an eye for the overlooked," says director Hal Brennan, whose previous film Northern Lights Café won the top prize at the Fjordline Film Festival. Solberg-Mbeki edited Brennan's new feature The Salt Road (2024), and her earlier editing credits include the documentaries Iron Shore (2019) and Saltmarsh (2023). She is based in Oslo.
