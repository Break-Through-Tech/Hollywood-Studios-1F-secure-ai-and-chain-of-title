Theo Marchetti is a New York-based stage and screen actor. On Broadway he originated the role of Ezra in The Lamplighters, and he has toured with the national company of Wren & Finch. On screen, he played Detective Sam Ruiz in the procedural series Harbor Precinct (2020–2023) and appeared as Marco in the feature A Late Frost (2019). Theo holds an MFA in Acting from Hollis Conservatory.
