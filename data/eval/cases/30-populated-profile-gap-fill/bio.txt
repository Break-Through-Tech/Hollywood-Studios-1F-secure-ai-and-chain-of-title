Sir Julian Ashcombe, founder of Larkspur Pictures, has produced film and television for more than thirty years. His feature credits as producer include Harbor Lights (2004) and Copper Season (2011), and he executive produced the limited series Northbound (2019). He also produced the feature Saint Clare's Hours (2014) and, most recently, executive produced the 2022 remake of Harbor Lights. He is represented by Dana Whitlock at Meridian Talent Group and is based in London.
