Valentina Ruiz-Ebrahimi is a Mexico City–based producer working across Spanish- and French-language cinema. She produced Les Éphémères (2020), a French-Mexican feature co-production, and was executive producer of the series La Casa del Faro (2021–2023). Her first feature as producer, El Último Verano de Tomás (2016), won the Premio Colibrí for Best First Film.
